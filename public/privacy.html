Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

The short version. Pepega Check has no server, no database and no analytics. It runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type or open is sent to us, because there is no “us” to send it to — we could not read your data even if we wanted to.

1. Who runs this site

Pepega Check is a free, personal, non-commercial hobby project maintained by an individual developer. It is an unofficial fan tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Warcraft Logs / RPGLogs, Wowhead / ZAM Network, or Blizzard Entertainment.

2. What the app is

Pepega Check is a static website: a bundle of HTML, CSS and JavaScript files served to your browser. All analysis of your Warcraft Logs reports happens locally, on your own device. There is no backend service that receives, processes or retains your data.

3. Data we collect

None. We operate no servers, run no analytics or tracking scripts, set no advertising cookies, and maintain no user accounts, profiles or mailing lists.

4. Data stored on your own device

The app uses your browser’s localStorage. This data never leaves your device except as described in section 5, and is readable only by this site in this browser.

Key What it holds Why
pepega-check.wcl-credentials Your Warcraft Logs API client ID and client secret, if you enter them So you do not have to re-enter them on every visit
pepega-check.wcl-token A short-lived Warcraft Logs access token and its expiry time To avoid requesting a new token on every page load

You can erase both at any time with ⚙ Settings → Clear all, or by clearing site data in your browser. Report contents are held in memory only and are gone as soon as you reload or close the tab.

Please note: because this is a static site with no backend, your API client secret is stored in your browser and used from your browser. Any browser extension with permission to read this page, or anyone with access to your device and browser profile, could read it. Treat it like a password, and prefer a dedicated Warcraft Logs API client for this app rather than one you use elsewhere.

5. Third parties your browser contacts

The app itself sends nothing anywhere, but loading and using it makes your browser talk directly to the services below. Each of them can see your IP address, your user agent and the page that referred you, and each has its own privacy policy that governs what it does with that. We receive none of this information.

Service What it is used for Its policy
warcraftlogs.com Fetching your report data, and exchanging your client credentials for an access token Warcraft Logs
assets.rpglogs.com Spell and ability icon images Warcraft Logs
wow.zamimg.com Wowhead’s tooltip script, which shows live spell tooltips when you hover an ability name. It may set its own cookies. Wowhead / ZAM
GitHub Pages Hosting the site’s files. GitHub logs requests, including IP addresses, as part of serving them. GitHub

Your Warcraft Logs client secret is sent only to warcraftlogs.com, over HTTPS, and only to obtain an access token. It is never sent to any other destination.

6. Shareable links

The app records what you are looking at — report code, boss, pull, selected raider — in the part of the address after the #. That makes a link shareable and a refresh land back where you were. Browsers do not transmit the part after the # to the web server, so it is not logged by our host. It is, however, plainly visible in the link itself: anyone you send a link to can see which report and pull it points at.

7. Report data and other people

A Warcraft Logs report describes a group of players. If you open a report, you are viewing data about your raid, not only yourself. That data comes from Warcraft Logs under your own API credentials and subject to their terms — we neither collect nor retain any of it. Please be considerate about sharing links to logs and analysis that name other people.

8. Children

This site is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Data-protection law gives you rights to access, correct, export and erase personal data held about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to produce or delete. The only data involved is on your own device, and you can erase it yourself at any time via ⚙ Settings → Clear all or by clearing site data in your browser. For the report data itself, exercise those rights with Warcraft Logs, who hold it.

10. How this app is built

Pepega Check is written with the help of AI coding assistants, alongside human review. We mention it because people reasonably want to know, and because it is easy to assume it means more than it does.

It changes nothing about your data. No AI service is part of the running app: there is no call to a model when you open a report, nothing you load is sent anywhere for analysis, and your credentials and log data never reach an AI provider. The assistance happens on the developer’s machine, while writing the source code, before anything is published. The published app is the plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript described in section 2, and its source is public if you want to check that for yourself.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it. Should the app ever gain a backend, accounts or paid features, this policy will be rewritten and the change noted here before those features go live.
